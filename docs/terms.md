## 4. Backups: please read

Syncing is **not a backup**. A sync can copy a change, including a deletion, a corrupted world or a
mistake, to your other computers. WorldHop keeps snapshots before overwriting and tries to avoid
data loss, but bugs, interrupted syncs, full disks, game or mod issues, and cloud problems can still
lose or damage worlds. **Keep your own independent backups of any world you care about, especially
before first use and before updates.**
